# 2026 course catalogue and pricing source

The website catalogue update is sourced from `docs/7th July 2026 The Toolkit brochure.pdf` (24 pages, dated 7 July 2026).

## Explicitly named pathways published separately

- Electrical Installation
- Solar PV Installer
- Solar Electrician
- Solar Upskilling
- Advanced Welding with VR Technology
- Advanced Welding Upskilling with VR
- Smart Agriculture
- French A1, A2 and B1
- German A1, A2 and B1

The brochure describes Entrepreneurship as a five-course suite, Digital Skills & Technology as six courses, and Smart Agriculture as having 11 additional specialisations, but does not name those individual courses. The website therefore retains Entrepreneurship and Digital Skills as suite pages and does not invent titles for unnamed programmes.

## Pricing publication control

The Toolkit Control screen includes a `2026 brochure pricing` toggle. It only takes effect while the 2026 catalogue toggle is enabled. When off, public course pages direct visitors to Admissions for current fees. When on, course pages display the brochure fee and any documented language package price.

The toggle must remain off until the approved effective date is confirmed. Constants in `wp-config.php` continue to take precedence over database options.
