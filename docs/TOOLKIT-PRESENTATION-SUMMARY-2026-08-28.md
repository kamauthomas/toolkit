# Toolkit Portfolio — Presentation Summary

**Date:** 28 August 2026  
**Audience:** Management and non-technical stakeholders  
**Scope:** Website, Reception, reporting, Smart Lecturer, Virtual Campus and communications assets

## Opening message

Toolkit is moving from separate manual processes to a connected set of services that make it easier to attract learners, welcome visitors, support teaching and account for daily work. The public website and Reception service are already in active use. The Virtual Campus has passed a supervised live-class trial and is the next major service to prepare for an approved pilot.

## What has been delivered

### 1. Public website

- Presents Toolkit courses, stories, testimonials, locations and contact information.
- Gives prospective learners a clearer path from discovering a course to making an enquiry or application.
- Uses the approved 2026 catalogue and pricing, with controlled updates where a price change still needs approval.
- Provides a stronger foundation for search visibility and trustworthy public information.

**Value:** More people can find accurate information without waiting for a staff member to answer every basic question.

### 2. Reception service

- Replaces paper-first visitor registration with a guided digital check-in.
- Supports QR entry, visitor details, course guidance and a clear reason when “Other” is selected.
- Keeps a record that staff can search and report on, while protecting sensitive details.
- Provides a separate, protected staff area for follow-up and administration.

**Value:** Less manual filing, fewer missing details and a more consistent welcome for every visitor.

### 3. Staff reporting

- Lets staff save a draft, submit a daily report and receive feedback.
- Gives managers a clear review and approval trail.
- Produces consistent summaries and printable reports for leadership.
- Includes a local admissions follow-up register with role-limited access,
  verification decisions and a visible history of changes.

**Value:** Leadership can see progress and follow-up needs without collecting scattered paper or chat messages.

### 4. Smart Lecturer

- Demonstrates a guided digital lecturer that answers approved Toolkit questions.
- Shows the answer in text, speech and captions, with visible supporting sources.
- Keeps the experience focused on learner-friendly explanations rather than unsupported claims.

**Value:** A useful demonstration of how learners could receive consistent guidance outside normal office hours.

### 5. Virtual Campus

- Provides course enrolment, lessons, assignments, feedback, announcements, discussions, results and certificates.
- Records meaningful learning activity instead of allowing progress to be claimed without evidence.
- Supports captioned lesson videos and a lower-data viewing option.
- Supports live online classes with a lecturer and learners, including camera, microphone, screen sharing, captions, participant roles and a clear way to leave class.
- Includes lecturer assessment authoring for controlled knowledge checks.

**Current position:** The live-class experience has been tested with one lecturer and two learners. It is ready for an approved pilot after the hosting, live-video, teaching-policy and support decisions are completed.

## Trust and continuity

- The website security incident was contained and investigated; normal user accounts were preserved.
- Sensitive applicant information is protected and access is limited to authorised staff.
- Staff areas are kept separate from public pages.
- Each service has a written owner-input list so decisions, credentials and approvals are not lost between teams.

## Communications and learning materials

- The approved Toolkit brand is recorded and reused across web, print and digital materials.
- The poster library contains 128 poster files plus editable sources and print-ready versions.
- The catalogue, prospectus, testimonials and campaign materials are retained in organised folders for future releases.

2. Provide the approved live-video service and campus hosting arrangements.
3. Confirm the final learner-support, attendance and data-retention rules.
4. Approve the email/WhatsApp notification owner and message templates for Reception.
5. Confirm the reporting modules leadership wants next: admissions follow-up, intake targets, meeting actions and reminders.
6. Approve the patched website theme release after the demo review.

## Suggested closing

The work is no longer a collection of isolated prototypes. The core public and reception journeys are operating, the reporting foundation is in place, and a supervised Virtual Campus class has worked end to end. The next step is disciplined piloting: agree the owners and policies, run with a small learner group, collect feedback and then expand with confidence.
