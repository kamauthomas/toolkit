# Toolkit Report System — Overview & Roles

**System:** Daily operations reporting for The Toolkit for Skills & Innovation
**Address:** reports.toolkitafrica.ac.ke
**Audience:** staff, department heads, and management
**Last updated:** 31 August 2026

---

## 1. What the system is for

The Report System is the institution's single place for **daily work reporting and
oversight**. Every member of staff files a short structured report of what they did
each day; department heads and management then see that activity rolled up into
dashboards, can review and sign off on individual reports, and can export the record
for meetings and audits.

It replaces scattered WhatsApp updates and end-of-week recall with one consistent,
searchable, timestamped record — so the institution always has an accurate picture of
who is doing what, where the work is concentrated, and what is outstanding.

---

## 2. What a report contains

Each daily report captures:

- **Report details** — date, day, reporting period, branch/location, department, position.
- **A brief summary** of the day.
- **Tasks completed** — one row per activity, with a description, time spent, and status
  (Completed / In progress).
- **Challenges** — what got in the way, its impact, the action taken, and any support needed.
- **Decisions** made and **comments**.
- **Plan for tomorrow** — the next day's intended tasks, owners, resources, and expected outcomes.
- **Department metrics** — a small set of numbers relevant to the reporter's department
  (for example: calls made and leads generated for Marketing; applications processed and
  interviews scheduled for Admissions; support tickets resolved and systems maintained for ICT).

On submission the system generates a **branded PDF** of the report automatically, so every
report has a shareable, print-ready copy.

---

## 3. The review workflow

Reports move through three states:

1. **Submitted** — the reporter has filed it (the default on submission).
2. **Reviewed** — a department head or manager has read it.
3. **Approved** — it has been signed off.

Reviewers change the status from the report view. Dashboards count how many reports sit in
each state so nothing is left unreviewed.

Reporters can also keep **drafts** and finish them later; drafts are private to the author
until submitted.

---

## 4. What everyone gets

Regardless of role, every signed-in user has:

- A personal **dashboard** with their own activity and trends.
- The ability to **file, edit, and export their own reports** (CSV) and generate report PDFs.
- **Drafts**, **notifications** (when their reports are reviewed or acted on), a **profile**,
  and self-service **password change**.

Account security is built in: repeated failed logins lock an account, and locked accounts
must be unlocked by a manager with a recorded reason.

The operations workspace also includes:

- admissions follow-up records with verification decisions and history;
- monthly intake targets compared with verified admissions;
- meeting minutes with responsible action owners, deadlines and progress states.
- monthly intake targets counted only from verified, fee-paid enrolments;
- incentive proposals with independent approval and payment references;
- configurable in-app reminder rules with delivery history.
- objectives and key results with accountable owners, calculated progress and
  an append-only evidence trail that can reference systems, reports, campaigns
  and poster deliverables;
- an approval-gated Wingu queue with manual or Excel attendance provenance and
  visible reconciliation status.
- a downloadable attendance workbook with row-level preview and deterministic
  matching to one approved report before any row enters that queue.

## 5. Roles

There are four roles. Each one includes everything the role below it can do, and adds more
oversight and control.

### 5.1 Employee

The default role for every staff member. New sign-ups (when self-registration is open) start
here.

An Employee can:

- File daily reports and save drafts.
- View, edit, and export **their own** reports, and download their PDFs.
- See their own dashboard, trends, and notifications.
- Manage their own profile and password.
- View OKRs in their department or assigned directly to them, and record
  evidence against key results they own.
- Queue their own approved daily report for Wingu using an authoritative
  attendance source.

An Employee sees **only their own** work — no one else's reports or figures.

### 5.2 Department Admin

A department head. Has everything an Employee has, plus oversight of **their own department**.

A Department Admin can:

- **Review and sign off** reports (move them Submitted → Reviewed → Approved) for the staff
  assigned to them.
- See a dashboard scoped to their assigned team's activity and metrics.
- Manage their **department's staff**: add employees to the department, activate/deactivate
  them, reset an employee's password, and unlock an inactivity-locked employee with a
  recorded reason.
- Set intake targets and coordinate meeting actions for staff assigned to them.
- Create and manage their department's OKRs and reconcile Wingu queue outcomes
  for reports in their authorised scope.
- Export their team's reports.

A Department Admin sees the reports of the staff **explicitly assigned to them**, and can only
manage accounts **below their own level** — they cannot act on peers or on management accounts.

### 5.3 Principal

An executive oversight role for institution-wide visibility, without day-to-day account
administration.

A Principal can:

- See **every department and every report** across the institution.
- Use the **executive overview** — the organisation-wide dashboard with per-department
  staffing, report volumes, review/approval rates, and rolled-up metrics.
- Review institution-wide admissions, intake targets, meetings and action follow-up.
- Review and sign off reports anywhere in the institution.
- Create and oversee institution-wide OKRs and view the full Wingu queue.
- Export the full report record.

A Principal is a **read-and-oversight** role: full visibility and sign-off, but no user
management, role changes, or system settings.

### 5.4 Super Admin

Full administrative control of the system. Has everything a Principal can do, plus complete
account and configuration management.

A Super Admin can:

- **Manage all users**: create accounts, change roles, activate/deactivate, and delete
  accounts (with a choice to keep or remove that person's report data).
- **Reset passwords** and **unlock** accounts for staff below management level.
- Assign which employees each Department Admin can see (**report access**).
- Open or close **self-registration**, and manage system **settings**.
- See and export **every report** across the institution.

For safety, management-level accounts are protected from each other: a Super Admin cannot
reset or take over another management account — only their own — so no single administrator
can seize control of another's account.

## 7. Notes for administrators

- **Self-registration** is controlled by the Super Admin. When open, new accounts are created
  as Employees and can be promoted afterwards.
- **Report access** determines which employees a Department Admin can see; it is set by a
  Super Admin. A Department Admin with no assignments sees only their own reports.
- **Account protection**: failed-login lockouts, recorded-reason unlocks, and the rule that
  management accounts cannot reset one another keep administrative control accountable.
  Employee inactivity locks remain enforced even if a role is changed; the account must use
  the explicit reasoned unlock path. The inactivity threshold is 14 days.
- **Data on account removal**: deleting a user offers a choice to keep their historical
  reports (for the institutional record) or remove them.
- **Wingu Box**: the internal approval-gated queue and reconciliation history
  are operational locally. External browser dispatch is not yet connected; it
  requires the approved Excel format and one human-authenticated discovery
  session. The canonical Excel format is now generated by the system and awaits
  owner approval. The safe procedure and remaining inputs are recorded in
  `WINGU_BOX_INTEGRATION_DESIGN.md` and `OWNER_INPUT_TRACKER.md`.
