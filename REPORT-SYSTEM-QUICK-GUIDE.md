# Toolkit Report System — Quick Guide

The Toolkit Report System at `reports.toolkitafrica.ac.ke` is the official place
for recording daily work. It gives staff one consistent record of completed
tasks, unfinished work, challenges, decisions, and the next day's plan. Managers
use the same records to review progress across staff and departments.

## Filing a daily report

1. Sign in with your own account and choose **New Report**.
2. Confirm the report date, branch, department, and position.
3. Write a short summary of the day.
4. Add each activity as a separate task, with the time spent and either
   **Completed** or **In progress** as its status.
5. Record challenges honestly: what happened, the effect it had, what action was
   taken, and what support is still needed.
6. Add decisions, useful comments, and the plan for the next working day.
7. Complete the department metrics that apply to your role, then save a draft or
   submit the report.

Submitted reports produce a branded PDF. A saved draft remains private to its
author until it is submitted.

## Review and access

- **Employees** file and manage their own reports.
- **Department Admins** review reports for staff assigned to their department.
- **Principals** have institution-wide visibility and sign-off access.
- **Super Admins** manage users, assignments, registration, and system settings.

Reports normally move from **Submitted** to **Reviewed** and then **Approved**.
Staff should check notifications for review activity or requested follow-up.

## Good reporting practice

Keep descriptions specific and measurable. State the result, not only the action;
for example, “Resolved the admissions form error and verified it on demo and
production” is more useful than “Worked on the website.” Use **In progress** when
work remains, name the blocker, and carry the next action into tomorrow's plan.

Never share passwords in a report. Avoid unnecessary personal or confidential
details; refer to protected records by their approved internal reference instead.
