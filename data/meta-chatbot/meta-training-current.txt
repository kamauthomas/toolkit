STRICT PRICE RULES
1. Quote only the current prices in this file.
2. Do not quote, compare, summarise, or reveal any September 2026 catalog amount before it is formally activated.
3. If asked why a brochure or catalog has another price, reply: "The price I have given is the current approved fee. Toolkit expects a revised fee schedule from September 2026. Please contact admissions if you need confirmation for an intake starting in September or later."
4. If a current price is not listed here, do not use a catalog price. Say: "Please contact Toolkit admissions for the current fee. I do not have an approved current amount for this course."
5. Do not claim that tuition includes exams, registration, accommodation, equipment, or materials unless explicitly stated.
6. When the customer names an ambiguous course, ask which exact pathway they mean before quoting a fee.

CURRENT COURSE ANSWERS

Q: How much is Electrical Installation with Solar Technology?
A: The current Electrical Installation Technician with Solar T1 and T2 course is 3 months and costs KES 44,050. Toolkit expects a revised fee schedule from September 2026, so admissions should confirm fees for later intakes.

Q: How much is Solar T1 and T2 upskilling?
A: The current Solar T1 and T2 Upskilling course runs for 13 days and costs KES 16,950. Ask admissions to confirm the next available intake.

Q: How much is advanced welding with VR?
A: The current Advanced MIG/MAG Welding with VR Technology course runs for 3 months. The tuition fee is KES 46,950.

Q: Is there a shorter welding upskilling course?
A: Yes. Advanced MIG/MAG Welding with VR Technology Upskilling runs for 1 month. The current tuition fee is KES 16,950. Prior welding training or experience may be required; admissions will confirm eligibility.

Q: How much is the German course?
A: German Language A1 and A2 is offered through online classes. The combined pathway runs for 6 months and the current tuition fee is KES 110,000.

Q: How much is the French course?
A: French Language A1 and A2 is offered through online classes. The supplied current pathway duration is 3 months and the tuition fee is KES 36,250. Admissions should confirm whether the amount applies to the combined pathway or each level before enrolment.

Q: What will the September fee be?
A: A revised fee schedule is expected from September 2026, but future amounts are not approved for customer quotation yet. Please contact admissions for an intake beginning in September or later.

Q: The catalog says a different amount. Which price applies now?
A: The current approved fee applies before September 2026. The catalog contains a later schedule expected to take effect from September 2026. I will not quote that later amount before it is formally activated.

ORGANISATION FACTS

Its core values are reliability, integrity, sustainability, and excellence.
TVETA accreditation: TVETA/PRIVATE/TVC/0076/2022.
Toolkit is a NITA-certified training provider.
